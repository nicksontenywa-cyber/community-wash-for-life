What We Do

Seven programmes, one shared mission

Our comprehensive programmes address the multifaceted challenges of water access, sanitation and community health.

Water

Water Access Projects

Building wells, rainwater harvesting systems and purification facilities in communities without access to clean water.

Well construction & rehabilitation
Solar-powered water systems
Community operator training
Sanitation

Sanitation Facilities

Constructing community toilets, waste management systems and promoting proper sanitation practices.

Community toilet blocks
Handwashing stations
Gender-segregated facilities
Hygiene

Hygiene Education

Training health workers and conducting workshops on handwashing, menstrual hygiene and disease prevention.

School & community workshops
MHM programmes for girls
Train-the-trainer approach
Community

Community Leadership

Developing local water committees and training technicians to maintain systems and promote lasting WASH practices.

Water committee formation
Technical skills training
Long-term sustainability plans
Health

Mental Health Support

Supporting community mental health with resources, counselling and resilience programmes — breaking the silence around depression.

Counselling & support groups
Volunteer facilitator training
Community awareness campaigns
Climate

Climate Adaptation

Helping communities adapt to climate change through water conservation, sustainable agriculture and disaster preparedness.

Water conservation techniques
Sustainable farming support
Disaster preparedness training
Education

Education & School WASH

Supporting schools with WASH facilities, scholarships, teacher training and learning materials so every child stays in class.

School WASH facility installation
Student scholarships
Teacher training workshops

Okello James
Headteacher · Banda Primary School

The mental health support programme helped our community address trauma and build real resilience. It showed us that true development is about the whole person — not just water infrastructure.

Nabirye Fatuma
Community Health Volunteer · Namayingo
