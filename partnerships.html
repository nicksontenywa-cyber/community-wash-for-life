Section 16 Framework

Our Four Partnership Pillars

Under Section 16 of the CLC Constitution, the organization actively builds cooperative relationships with entities whose activities align with our mission, safeguarding guidelines, and legal character:

1. Christian Churches

Local Christian congregations in Busia District collaborate to mobilize volunteers, provide spiritual mentorship, and connect isolated community members to caring church homes.

2. Government Institutions

Working in synergy with the Busia District Local Government, Community Development Officers (CDOs), Probation & Social Welfare, Police CFPU, and health inspectorates.

3. Development Partners

Philanthropic organizations, foundations, and institutional donors who provide lawful grants and technical resources under rigorous financial accountability.

4. Community Organizations

Local schools, women's saving associations, disability advocate groups, and health centers partnering for specialized referral and community-wide dialogue.
