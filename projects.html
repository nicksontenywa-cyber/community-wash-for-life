Active Field Initiatives

Our Community Projects

Grassroots initiatives meeting physical, emotional, and spiritual needs across Busia District.

Active Project

Busia Household Listening Outreach

Mobilizing 120 certified Christian Community Listeners across 7 sub-counties to visit households, listen to family burdens without judgment, offer prayer, and identify vulnerable cases.

  • Over 1,850 households reached to date
  • Sub-counties: Banda, Dabani, Lunyo, Nahayima
View Program Details
Schools Initiative

School Youth Character & Life Skills

Providing weekly moral mentorship, life skills seminars, and academic encouragement for candidate students across 15 primary and secondary schools in Busia.

  • 1,200+ students actively mentored
  • Peer listening clubs established
View Program Details
Peace Initiative

Restorative Family Reconciliation

A community dialogue initiative facilitating non-adversarial, restorative mediation for broken homes, marital conflict, and village land disputes.

  • 45+ domestic disputes peacefully resolved
  • Partnering with Local Council leaders
