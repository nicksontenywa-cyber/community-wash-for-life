Our Story

Clean water & education
for every child in Namayingo.

New Grace JS is a global charity committed to addressing the critical need for clean water, sanitation, hygiene (WASH) and quality education in underserved communities. Founded in 2018 in Uganda, we operate as a community-based organisation in Namayingo and Busia Districts.

Our approach combines infrastructure development with community education and empowerment. We believe access to clean water and education are fundamental human rights — the foundation for health, economic development and sustainable communities.

Registered under number BAS/CS/4067 on 4th December 2020, we work from Banda Sub County, Buchumba Parish, Namayingo District, Uganda.

Our Journey

Six years of growing impact

2018

New Grace JS Founded

Isaac Sifuna establishes New Grace JS in Busuma Village, Banda Subcounty with a focus on community-led WASH education.

2020

Official Registration

Formally registered as a community-based organisation under BAS/CS/4067 on 4th December 2020, unlocking donor partnerships.

2021

First School WASH Program

Launched hygiene education in 5 primary schools, installing handwashing stations and reaching 1,500 children in year one.

2022

First Solar Borehole

Installed our first solar-powered borehole serving 200 families, reducing waterborne disease by 70% in the first six months.

2023

Women's Empowerment Launch

Established women's savings groups and livelihood training — 30 women launched a soap-making cooperative with seed funding.

2024–25

5,000 People Reached

Expanded to 20+ schools and completed 8 boreholes across Namayingo District — reaching over 5,000 community members.
