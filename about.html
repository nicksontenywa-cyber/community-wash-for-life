About Community Listeners for Christ

“Listening with Love, Sharing Christ’s Hope.”

Section 1 & 2

Organization Overview & Legal Identity

Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) is a Christian, non-profit and non-partisan Community-Based Organization established to promote compassionate listening, spiritual encouragement, responsible referral, family strengthening and holistic support for individuals and families within communities.

CLC brings together Christian volunteers who are committed to serving communities through listening, prayer, encouragement, mentorship, practical care and connection to appropriate support services. We recognize that individuals and families experience social, emotional, spiritual, and psychosocial challenges that require compassionate attention. CLC mobilizes trained community volunteers who can listen responsibly, identify needs within their competence, and refer cases requiring specialized assistance to qualified providers.

Section 3

Our Vision

“Christ-centered communities where every person experiences compassionate listening, prayer, dignity, support and hope.”

Section 4

Our Mission

“To equip and mobilize Christian volunteers to listen compassionately, provide appropriate spiritual encouragement, promote positive family and community relationships, support vulnerable people through responsible referral and partnership, and contribute to the holistic well-being of individuals and families.”

Section 5

Our 9 Core Values

The foundational principles governing every interaction, visit, and program of CLC:

1. Christian Love

CLC seeks to demonstrate Christian love through compassionate listening, prayer, unhurried encouragement, and selfless service.

2. Compassion

CLC values deep empathy and seeks to respond to people experiencing trauma, grief, and hardship with heartfelt understanding and care.

3. Dignity and Respect

Every human person is created in the image of God and must be treated with absolute dignity, honour, and unconditional respect.

4. Integrity

CLC promotes honesty, moral uprightness, accountability, transparency, and responsible stewardship across all activities.

5. Confidentiality

Information obtained through listening, prayer, or home visits is handled responsibly and confidentially, subject only to safeguarding laws.

6. Service

CLC encourages Christian volunteers to serve their communities with humility, commitment, responsibility, and servant-leadership.

7. Safeguarding

Committed to protecting children, young people, persons with disabilities, and vulnerable persons from abuse, exploitation, and harm.

8. Inclusion

Services are delivered respectfully and without coercion. Participation is never conditional upon conversion or religious affiliation.

9. Accountability

Dedicated to transparent governance, responsible financial management, open records, and regular reporting to the General Assembly.

Sections 6 & 7

Goal & Constitutional Objectives

Overall Organizational Goal

To contribute to Christ-centered, caring and supportive communities where individuals and families can access compassionate listening, spiritual encouragement, appropriate support, responsible referral and opportunities for positive relationships and community wellbeing.

9 Specific Constitutional Objectives:

1

To train Christian volunteers in active listening, biblical counselling within appropriate limits, discipleship, safeguarding and responsible referral.

2

To conduct household and community visits for prayer, encouragement, listening and appropriate psychosocial or spiritual support.

3

To strengthen positive relationships between churches and surrounding communities through service.

4

To mentor children and young people in Christian values, life skills, responsible citizenship and leadership.

5

To support vulnerable individuals and families through appropriate referrals, practical care and partnerships with churches, government and other community organizations.

6

To promote peace, forgiveness, reconciliation, family strengthening and community unity.

7

To promote safeguarding, confidentiality, dignity and protection of children and other vulnerable persons.

8

To mobilize lawful resources and partnerships to implement approved community activities.

9

To undertake other lawful activities consistent with the organization's mission and objectives, subject to applicable approvals.

Section 21

Non-Profit & Non-Political Character

CLC operates strictly as a non-profit and non-partisan entity. It does not distribute income, property, or surpluses as dividends to members or founders. Any surplus is reinvested exclusively in community programs.

CLC does not affiliate with, campaign for, or donate to any political party or political candidate.

Section 24

Long-Term Strategic Direction

CLC seeks to develop a strong, responsible network of Christian Community Listeners across Busia District and surrounding areas:

Christian volunteers → Community listening → Family support → Youth mentorship → Responsible referral → Community unity → Holistic wellbeing

Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) is a Christian faith-based, non-profit, non-partisan CBO operating in Busia District, Uganda. We listen with love, share Christ's hope, and build resilient communities.
