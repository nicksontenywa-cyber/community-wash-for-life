Community Testimonials

Voices of Hope & Reconciliation

Real testimonies from households, youth, and community leaders touched by Christian Community Listeners in Busia District.

“My home was on the brink of divorce. A neighbor told me about Community Listeners for Christ. Two trained volunteers visited us weekly. They created a safe atmosphere where my wife and I could speak without shouting. They prayed with us and helped us forgive one another.”

DM
David M.
Father of Three · Nahayima Road Community

“When I was struggling with school fees anxiety and depression before my P7 exams, the CLC youth mentors came to our school club. They encouraged us from God’s Word, gave us notebooks, and listened to our fears. I passed with Division One because my mind found peace.”

GK
Grace K.
Student Candidate · Busia Town

“As a village Local Council Chairman, I have seen many land and family disputes that police couldn't settle. CLC’s listeners approached these families with restorative Christian dialogue and patience. They have restored peace to several homes without court drama.”

LC
Local Council Chairperson
Banda Sub-County

“When my mother fell ill with chronic diabetes, we didn’t know how to navigate the district hospital. The Community Listener who visited prayed with us and personally escorted us through the referral system to the health center doctor. That guidance was a true gift from God.”

SW
Stephen W.
Family Caregiver · Dabani Sub-County

“Being a widow in rural Busia can make you feel completely forgotten. But the volunteer listeners from CLC visit me regularly. They never rush away. They listen to my old stories, pray over my home, and help me feel that God still sees and loves me.”

EN
Esther N.
Elderly Widow · Lunyo Sub-County

“Our church has partnered with CLC to train our congregation members as community listeners. It has transformed how our believers engage the village. Instead of arguing about doctrine, our people are now known as humble, loving listeners who help families in crisis.”

BP
Rev. Pastor
Busia Town Baptist Church Fellowship
