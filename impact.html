Section 22 Deliverables

Expected Contributions to Communities

The verified constitutional outcomes Community Listeners for Christ actively produces across Busia District:

1. Greater Access to Listening

Every individual in participating sub-counties can easily request a confidential listening visit without judgment or fee.

2. Spiritual Encouragement & Support

Sharing God's love, hope in trials, and gentle pastoral care respectfully to all desiring prayer.

3. Stronger Family Relationships

Mitigating marital tension, teaching peaceful dialogue, and strengthening parents in loving child care.

4. Youth Mentorship & Development

Equipping school candidates and out-of-school youths with life skills, leadership, and Christian character.

5. Better Identification & Referral

Connecting hidden vulnerable individuals to healthcare centers, social welfare officers, and legal clinics.

6. Church–Community Ties

Bridges built between local congregations and their surrounding secular neighbors through genuine selfless service.

7. Protection of Children & Vulnerable

Heightened community vigilance against child abuse, neglect, and property exploitation of widows.

8. Peace, Forgiveness & Reconciliation

Active dispute mediation resolving long-standing boundary feuds and family grievances restoratively.

9. Stronger Community Unity

Fostering harmonious neighborliness and collective mutual aid across clan and cultural groups.

10. Holistic Wellbeing

Promoting physical, emotional, spiritual, and relational flourishing for every family member.
