Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our organization, programs, and how you can support us.

Common Questions

Who is New Grace JS and what does the organization do?
New Grace JS is a global charity founded in 2018, dedicated to providing clean water, sanitation, hygiene (WASH), quality education, and community empowerment in underserved areas. We work primarily in Uganda with local expertise and international partnerships to implement sustainable solutions that transform lives.
Is New Grace JS registered as a charitable organization?
Yes, we are registered as a Community-Based Organization in Uganda with Registration Number BAS/CS/4067 as of December 4, 2020. Our operations are based in Banda Sub County, Busia District, Uganda, with a focus on serving Namayingo and Busia communities.
What are the core values of New Grace JS?
Our core values include: Integrity (transparency and ethical actions), Innovation (embracing new ideas), Community Empowerment (working hand-in-hand with communities), and Collaboration (partnering with multiple stakeholders). These guide all our actions and decisions.
How can I donate to New Grace JS?
You can donate through our website at donate.html. We accept donations via MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and ATM cards. You can make a one-time donation or become a monthly supporter. Visit our donate page for detailed instructions for each payment method.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
As a registered Community-Based Organization in Uganda, we are committed to transparency and proper financial management. For tax deductibility information in your country, please contact us directly at newgracejs@gmail.com or call 0709251412.
Where does my donation go?
Your donations directly support our programs: water and sanitation projects, education initiatives, health education, and community empowerment programs. We maintain a high program ratio, ensuring most of your donation reaches community projects. See our impact page for detailed statistics.
What are the main programs offered by New Grace JS?
Our main programs include: Quality Education (formal education support, teacher training, vocational skills), Water & Sanitation (water access projects, sanitation facilities, hygiene education), Health & Hygiene (disease prevention, nutrition programs), and Community Leadership (livelihood skills, women's groups, microfinance).
How many people has New Grace JS helped?
To date, we have reached over 5,000 community members, educated 1,200+ school children, supported 20+ local schools, and completed dozens of water and sanitation projects. Our work continues to expand as we secure more partnerships and resources.
How are projects selected and prioritized?
Projects are selected through community consultations where we identify the most pressing needs. We prioritize based on community demand, organizational capacity, available resources, and potential for long-term sustainable impact. Community participation and ownership are essential in all project selection.
How can I volunteer with New Grace JS?
We have many volunteer opportunities available! Visit our volunteer page to see current opportunities, including education, water projects, health education, and digital support roles. Complete our volunteer application form, and our team will review your application and contact you with next steps.
Do I need experience to volunteer?
While some positions require specific skills or experience, we welcome volunteers of all skill levels. We provide training and support to help you succeed in your role. Your commitment and willingness to learn are most important.
Can I partner with New Grace JS for corporate social responsibility?
Absolutely! We welcome corporate partnerships and CSR collaborations. Organizations can support specific projects, provide volunteer time, offer in-kind contributions, or sponsor initiatives. Contact us at newgracejs@gmail.com to discuss partnership opportunities that align with your organization's goals.
