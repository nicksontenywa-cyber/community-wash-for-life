Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent explanations about our faith identity, free services, ethical boundaries, safeguarding, and volunteering.

1. What is Community Listeners for Christ (CLC)?

CLC is a Christian, non-profit, non-partisan Community-Based Organization (CBO) operating in Busia District, Uganda (Nahayima Road). We mobilize trained Christian volunteers to provide unhurried, compassionate listening, spiritual encouragement, family strengthening, youth mentorship, and responsible referral to specialized care.

2. Are CLC listening visits and community services free?

Yes, 100% free of charge. No volunteer or officer of CLC is permitted to charge fees, solicit personal gifts, or demand favors from individuals or families being visited. All resources are mobilized ethically through approved donor and member contributions.

3. Do I have to be a Christian to receive support from CLC?

No. Under Section 11 of our Constitution (Inclusion Principle), while CLC is Christian in identity, our community services are delivered respectfully and without coercion. Participation in CLC services is never conditional upon conversion or religious affiliation. We serve every person with dignity and Christian love.

4. What is the difference between a Community Listener and a Psychiatrist/Counselor?

A Community Listener is a compassionate Christian neighbor trained in active listening, emotional accompaniment, and prayer. Community Listeners do NOT diagnose psychiatric disorders, prescribe medication, or provide formal psychotherapy. Whenever clinical, medical, legal, or severe mental health conditions are detected, our volunteers strictly refer the person to qualified doctors, psychiatric facilities, or legal authorities.

5. What happens to personal information shared during a listening visit?

Under Section 13, all disclosures are held in strict confidence. Volunteers do not share family matters with neighbors, friends, or churches. The only exception is where there is imminent danger to life (e.g., suicide, severe domestic violence, or child abuse), in which case immediate reports are made to authorized safeguarding authorities and police.

6. Who are CLC's primary institutional partners?

Our primary strategic community partner is Resolution, and our principal faith and ministry partner is Busia Town Baptist Church. We also work closely with Busia District Local Government departments, local schools, health centers, and community church fellowships.

7. Is CLC involved in politics or affiliated with political parties?

No. Section 21 of our Constitution expressly mandates that CLC is strictly non-political and non-partisan. Organizational resources, meetings, or volunteers may not campaign for or against any political candidate or party.

8. How can I submit a complaint or report a volunteer misconduct concern?

Under Section 20, you can submit confidential feedback directly online through our Safeguarding & Complaints form on the contact page or via email at safeguarding@clcuganda.org. All reports are investigated independently with guaranteed whistleblower protection.
