Organizational Overview

A Christ-Centered Heart for Busia’s Communities

Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) is a Christian, non-profit and non-partisan Community-Based Organization established to promote compassionate listening, spiritual encouragement, responsible referral, family strengthening and holistic support for individuals and families in Busia District.

Our Vision

“Christ-centered communities where every person experiences compassionate listening, prayer, dignity, support and hope.”

Our Mission

“To equip and mobilize Christian volunteers to listen compassionately, provide appropriate spiritual encouragement, promote positive family and community relationships, support vulnerable people through responsible referral and partnership, and contribute to the holistic well-being of individuals and families.”

Faith Identity & Inclusion

CLC is proudly guided by Christian values while ensuring services are delivered respectfully and without coercion. Participation in CLC services is never conditional upon conversion or religious affiliation. We honor every person with love and dignity.

Core Methodology

The Community Listener Model

The Community Listener is central to CLC’s approach. Our volunteers are equipped to provide safe, structured, community-level care while maintaining strict professional boundaries. Click any step below to explore its field practice:

1

LISTEN

Unhurried, compassionate, non-judgmental presence in households.

2

UNDERSTAND

Discerning underlying emotional, family, or spiritual burdens.

3

ENCOURAGE

Uplifting biblical encouragement & prayer where welcomed.

4

SUPPORT

Practical care, family dialogue & youth life skills accompaniment.

5

REFER

Mandatory referral for specialized medical, legal, or child protection cases.

6

FOLLOW UP

Continuous care continuity, family check-ins & walking alongside.

Step 1: LISTEN (Compassionate & Non-Judgmental Presence)

Christian Community Listeners visit households and community members to offer patient, dignified, and active listening. We provide a safe, calm space for people to express grief, worry, marital tension, or life challenges without fear of judgment, criticism, or unwanted unsolicited advice.

Field Action: Unhurried home visits, listening with deep empathy, active reflective listening, and unconditional positive regard.

Strict Boundary Rule (Section 9 & 13) Boundary: Listeners never diagnose, dispute, or force conversation; listening is confidential and completely voluntary. Specialized medical, legal, or child protection needs must be referred.
