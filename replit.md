# Community Listeners for Christ (CLC)

## Overview
Official website for **Community Listeners for Christ (CLC)**, a Christian, non-profit and non-partisan Community-Based Organization (CBO) based on Nahayima Road, Busia District, Uganda.

- **Theme / Slogan:** *“Listening with Love, Sharing Christ’s Hope.”*
- **Area of Operation:** Busia District, Uganda (Sub-county level and below).
- **Core Model:** The 6-Step Community Listener Model (`LISTEN → UNDERSTAND → ENCOURAGE → SUPPORT → REFER → FOLLOW UP`).
- **Core Partners:** 
  - **Resolution** (Strategic Community Partner)
  - **Busia Town Baptist Church** (Faith & Ministry Partner)
  - Busia District Local Government & Healthcare Stakeholders

## 7 Core Program Departments
1. **Community Listening & Spiritual Encouragement** (Household visits, active listening, prayer, follow-ups)
2. **Volunteer Training & Capacity Building** (Active listening, biblical limits, safeguarding, referral protocols)
3. **Family Strengthening** (Marital dialogue, domestic peacebuilding, forgiveness, reconciliation)
4. **Children & Youth Mentorship** (Life skills, leadership, Christian values, candidate school outreaches)
5. **Support to Vulnerable Individuals & Families** (Practical care for elderly, widows, orphans, and persons with disabilities)
6. **Peace, Reconciliation & Community Unity** (Dispute mitigation, community cohesion, restorative forums)
7. **Church–Community Engagement** (Mobilizing volunteer teams, demonstrating faith through service)

## Project Structure
- `index.html` - Homepage featuring interactive 6-step model explorer, 7 program areas, stats, real community photos, partners, and modals.
- `about.html` - Comprehensive Constitution-aligned profile (Overview, Vision, Mission, 9 Core Values, 9 Objectives, Legal character).
- `services.html` / `programes.html` - Detailed breakdown of the 7 Program Areas & the Community Listener Model.
- `community-leadership.html` - Governance structure (General Assembly, Executive Committee, 6 Sub-Committees, Team profiles).
- `safeguarding.html` - Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy, Confidentiality, and Responsible Referral Pathways (Health, Legal, Police CFPU).
- `partnerships.html` - Strategic Alliances spotlighting Resolution and Busia Town Baptist Church.
- `volunteer.html` - Volunteer as a Christian Community Listener (Role, Code of Conduct, Interactive Application Form).
- `impact.html` / `testimonials.html` - Section 22 Expected Community Contributions and verified community stories from Busia sub-counties.
- `gallery.html` - Filterable photo gallery with authentic field photographs.
- `donate.html` - Sections 17 & 18 Resource Mobilization & Financial Accountability (Mobile Money MTN/Airtel, Bank Wire, Pesapal).
- `contact.html` - Physical office details on Nahayima Road, Busia, with Prayer/Listening Request Form and Section 20 Independent Feedback/Complaints Form.
- `faq.html` - Frequently Asked Questions.
- `resources.html` - Official downloadable/viewable documentation.
- `events.html` - Upcoming training seminars and peace forums.
- `blog.html` - Ministry updates and field insights.
- `styles.css` - Master stylesheet with deep forest green, emerald, gold, and warm parchment tokens.
- `main.js` - Interactive scripts: sticky navbar, mobile drawer, 6-step model switcher, animated counters, modals, toasts, tabs.
- `server.js` - Express backend with Pesapal payment integration.
- `server.py` - Lightweight Python development server.

## Running the Project
```bash
# Node / Express Server (Port 3000)
node server.js

# Python Development Server (Port 5000)
python3 server.py
```
