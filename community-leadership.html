Constitution Section 14

Governance & Leadership Structure

Accountable, transparent, and servant-hearted oversight ensuring ethical Christian stewardship and community protection.

Governance Hierarchy

Constitutional Decision-Making Structure

Community Listeners for Christ operates under a three-tiered governance system mandated by our Constitution to ensure accountability, statutory compliance, and transparent financial stewardship:

Section 14.1

The General Assembly

The supreme decision-making organ of CLC, comprising all members in good standing. It meets annually to determine long-term direction, review audits, and elect committee officers.

  • Reviewing & adopting annual reports
  • Approving audited financial reports
  • Approving annual work plans & budgets
  • Electing Executive Committee officers
  • Constitutional amendments & major decisions
Section 14.2

The Executive Committee

Governs the organization between General Assembly sessions. Responsible for strategic management, project supervision, legal compliance, and stakeholder partnerships.

  • Chairperson (Strategic & spiritual oversight)
  • Vice Chairperson (Deputizing & operational coordination)
  • Secretary (Records, minutes & statutory notices)
  • Treasurer (Books of account & bank management)
  • Publicity / Mobilization Secretary
  • Two Committee Members (Community voice)
Section 14.3

Specialized Sub-Committees

Operational committees reporting to the Executive Committee, driving technical implementation, volunteer training, safeguarding, and field quality control.

  • Finance Sub-Committee (Budget review & audits)
  • Training Sub-Committee (Volunteer listener curriculum)
  • Safeguarding Sub-Committee (Child protection & ethics)
  • Youth Mentorship Sub-Committee (Schools & clubs)
  • Prayer & Spiritual Support (Pastoral encouragement)
  • Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) (Impact tracking)
Servant Leadership

Executive Officers & Field Coordinators

Dedicated Christian servants leading CLC's mission across Busia District with integrity and compassion:

Executive Chairperson

Executive Committee

Providing spiritual and strategic governance, presiding over the General Assembly, and championing the culture of compassionate listening across Busia District.

Executive Secretary & Director

Executive Committee

Overseeing day-to-day operations, official correspondence, partnership liaisons, statutory filings, and field team alignment with the Constitution.

Treasurer & Records Officer

Executive Committee

Safeguarding financial records, maintaining authorized bank accounts, ensuring transparent voucher accountability, and presenting annual audits.

Community Field Officer

Publicity & Mobilization

Leading community entry, coordinating sub-county household visits, liaising with Local Councils, and managing volunteer deployment in rural zones.

Section 15

Membership Structure

Membership in Community Listeners for Christ is organized into three constitutional categories:

1. Founding Members

Individuals who initiated the establishment of CLC, subscribed to its founding constitution, and laid its operational foundations.

2. Ordinary / Volunteer Members

Active Christian Community Listeners who have undergone certified training, signed the Safeguarding Pledge, and serve in field programs.

3. Honorary Members

Persons recognized by the General Assembly for distinguished Christian service, wise counsel, or notable contributions to community wellbeing.

Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) operates with constitutional accountability and Christian integrity in Busia District, Uganda.
