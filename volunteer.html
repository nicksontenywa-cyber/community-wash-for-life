Open Roles

Current volunteer opportunities

We have opportunities for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

Education Community Educator

Teach hygiene and education programmes in local schools and communities across Namayingo District.

Time: 2–3 days/week
Location: Busia District
Duration: 6+ months
Skills: Teaching experience, passion for education

Water Project Assistant

Assist in water system installation, maintenance and community training for boreholes and purification units.

Time: Flexible
Location: Field-based
Duration: 3–12 months
Skills: Technical skills, physical fitness

Health Peer Educator

Conduct health and sanitation education workshops in communities and health centres across Namayingo.

Time: Flexible
Location: Community centres
Duration: Ongoing
Skills: Health knowledge, communication

Digital & Social Media Volunteer

Manage social media, create content and support digital communications — this role is fully remote.

Time: 5–10 hrs/week
Location: Remote
Duration: 6+ months
Skills: Social media, content creation

Monitoring & Evaluation Volunteer

Support data collection and monitoring of programme impact and outcomes in the field and office.

Time: Part or full-time
Location: Office & Field
Duration: 6+ months
Skills: Analytical, data collection

Translator / Interpreter

Bridge communication between international partners and local communities in Lusoga, Samia, English and Swahili.

Time: Project-based
Location: Field-based
Duration: Flexible
Skills: Fluency in local languages & English
