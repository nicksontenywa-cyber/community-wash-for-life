Christian Community Service

Become a Community Listener

Join a dedicated fellowship of Christian volunteers trained to visit homes, listen with compassion, and share Christ’s hope across Busia District.

The Calling

Who is a Community Listener?

A Community Listener is a Christian volunteer equipped to serve individuals and families through active listening, prayer, encouragement, mentorship, practical care, and responsible connection to appropriate support services.

Many people in our villages carry heavy grief, domestic burdens, or spiritual anxiety in isolation. They do not need quick answers, arguments, or condemnation; they need someone who will sit quietly, listen with the heart of Jesus, and stand with them in prayer and dignity.

Volunteer Eligibility:

  • Mature Christian faith with a servant's heart
  • Resident of Busia District or serving local church
  • Willingness to complete 4-week Listening & Safeguarding Course
  • Clean safeguarding record and pastoral recommendation
  • Commitment to unconditional respect and confidentiality

What You WILL Do

  • Visit households with gentleness and unhurried patience
  • Listen without interrupting or passing judgment
  • Offer comforting prayer where welcomed by the family
  • Affirm human dignity and share Christian hope
  • Safely refer specialized medical, psychiatric, or legal issues
  • Protect children and report safeguarding concerns

What You WILL NOT Do

  • Do NOT give medical diagnosis or prescribe medicine
  • Do NOT practice clinical psychotherapy or psychiatric counseling
  • Do NOT coerce anyone into religion or condition aid on conversion
  • Do NOT gossip, share secrets, or disclose names to neighbors
  • Do NOT accept personal loans, gifts, or financial entanglements
  • Do NOT engage in partisan politics or campaign for candidates
