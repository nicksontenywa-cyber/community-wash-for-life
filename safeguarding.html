Constitution Sections 12 & 13

Safeguarding, Confidentiality & Referrals

Our unwavering commitment to protecting children, vulnerable adults, and respecting professional boundaries in Busia District.

Section 12

Safeguarding and Protection Policy

Safeguarding is a fundamental and non-negotiable part of CLC's work. As a Christian community organization, we are mandated to protect the vulnerable from any form of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or harm.

Whom We Protect:

Children

Zero tolerance for child labor, neglect, physical or emotional abuse.

Young People

Protection from sexual exploitation, coercion, and harmful cultural practices.

Persons with Disabilities

Ensuring dignified accessibility, safety, and proactive advocacy.

Vulnerable Adults

Protection for isolated elderly persons and widows from property grabbing or neglect.

Volunteer Code of Conduct Mandates:

  • Volunteers must never be alone in closed spaces with children without parental consent or adult supervision.
  • Volunteers must immediately report any suspected child abuse to the Safeguarding Sub-Committee and Child & Family Protection Unit (CFPU).
  • No volunteer may accept financial gifts, personal favors, or promises that compromise ethical neutrality.
  • Every volunteer signs an annual legally binding Safeguarding & Code of Conduct agreement before field deployment.
Section 13

Confidentiality & Responsible Referral Pathways

The Community Listener model is built on mutual trust. Information shared during listening visits is treated with absolute confidentiality, with clear, legal boundaries for safety:

Strict Confidentiality

What is shared in a home visit stays strictly confidential. Volunteers do not discuss family issues with neighbors, church congregations, or friends. Personal records and case notes are stored securely under password-protected and locked custody.

Limits to Confidentiality

Confidentiality must be broken ONLY when there is imminent risk of suicide, physical violence, acute child sexual abuse, or where disclosure is legally required by Uganda statutory authorities. Even then, reporting is done through authorized safeguarding protocols.

Official Responsible Referral Pathways

Volunteers strictly recognize their limits and immediately connect cases requiring specialized intervention:

Medical & Health

Referred to Busia Health Center IV, District Hospitals, or certified clinical centers.

Psychiatric & Mental Health

Referred to certified clinical psychologists and psychiatric nurses. No amateur therapy.

Child Protection

Immediate escalation to Police Child & Family Protection Unit (CFPU) and District Probation Officer.

Legal & Domestic Violence

Referred to legal aid clinics, Local Council Courts, and authorized justice institutions.

Section 20

Independent Accountability & Complaints Mechanism

To ensure fairness and prevent abuse of power, Section 20 of our Constitution establishes a transparent complaints procedure:

1. Who Can Report:

Members, volunteers, beneficiaries, local elders, and community stakeholders can lodge concerns without fear.

2. Fair & Independent Review:

Where a complaint concerns an Executive Committee officer, that individual is recused and barred from investigating.

3. Non-Retaliation Guarantee:

Whistleblowers and complainants are legally protected from retaliation, intimidation, or exclusion.

Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) upholds child protection, human dignity, and transparent governance in Busia District, Uganda.
