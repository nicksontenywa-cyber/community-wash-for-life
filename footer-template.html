Community Listeners for Christ (CLC) is a Christian faith-based, non-profit, non-partisan Community-Based Organization operating in Busia District, Uganda. We equip Christian volunteers to listen with love, share Christ's hope, strengthen families, mentor youth, and responsibly connect vulnerable neighbors to appropriate care.
