Sections 17 & 18 Accountability

Support Our Community Mission

Every shilling directly equips Christian volunteers to reach isolated homes, strengthen families, mentor youth, and share Christ’s hope in Busia District.

Our Constitutional Stewardship & Non-Profit Pledge

Under Sections 18 and 21 of our Constitution, CLC operates under strict financial accountability: all funds are held in an official organizational bank account requiring dual authorized signatories. Zero profits or dividends are ever distributed. Financial statements are presented annually to the General Assembly and submitted to statutory regulators.

How Your Gift Transforms Lives

Transparent breakdown of what your charitable contributions achieve on the ground:

50,000 UGX Listener Field Pack

Provides a Christian volunteer listener with field transportation, casebook, and Bible for 15 household visits.

150,000 UGX Family Reconciliation Forum

Sponsors a restorative dialogue session for estranged families and village neighbors with trained Christian mediators.

300,000 UGX School Youth Life Skills Summit

Funds a full-day character mentorship and leadership workshop for 80 young candidates in a local school.
