Section 8.4

Equipping Youth with Character & Hope

CLC recognizes that youth and children face intense social pressures, poverty, academic anxiety, and negative cultural influences. Our Children & Youth Mentorship department provides safe, encouraging environments where young people are heard, guided, and grounded in Christian values.

  • Christian Values: Integrity, honesty, respect for parents, and servant leadership.
  • Practical Life Skills: Decision-making, peer pressure resistance, conflict resolution.
  • School Outreach: Academic encouragement and candidate exam prayer seminars.
  • Safeguarding & Protection: Zero tolerance for child exploitation and child abuse.
