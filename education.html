Our Education Mission

Four pillars of educational empowerment

New Grace JS believes quality education is the foundation for breaking the cycle of poverty. We provide formal education, vocational training, and community education programmes designed to empower individuals and strengthen communities across Namayingo.

🎓

Formal Education Support

We support quality formal education for marginalised children through school partnerships, teacher training, and provision of educational materials and infrastructure.

🔧

Vocational Training

Practical skills training in trades and crafts to help youth and adults gain sustainable livelihoods and economic independence in their communities.

👥

Community Education

Adult literacy programmes, health education, WASH training and community awareness initiatives that strengthen social fabric and empower communities.

📚

Resource Provision

Supplying schools with libraries, learning materials, digital resources and technology access to improve educational quality and measurable outcomes.

Featured Impact

School hygiene clubs changing behaviour — for life.

In 2021, New Grace JS launched hygiene education clubs across 10 primary schools in Namayingo District. Within two terms, headteachers reported that absenteeism due to diarrhoeal illness had dropped by more than half.

Children are now handwashing ambassadors in their own homes — carrying health knowledge back to their families and villages. The clubs now run independently, led by trained pupil health leaders.

Education + WASH

Where education meets clean water

WASH and education go hand in hand. Clean facilities, healthy children, better learning outcomes — every time.

🚰

Clean Water in Schools

Every partner school receives a safe water point — reducing class time lost to water collection and illness, keeping children present and focused.

↑ 40% school attendance
🚻

Girl-Friendly Sanitation

Separate, lockable latrines and menstrual hygiene kits keep adolescent girls in school every day of the month — not just three weeks out of four.

90 girls sponsored
🧼

Handwashing Stations

We installed 45 handwashing stations across supported schools, backed by hygiene education training for both teachers and students.
